Etsy profit calculator for digital products

Tick "Digital product". Shipping and materials go to zero, so your costs are the listing fee, the 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing. One digital listing can sell many times; the listing fee is charged again on each sale (auto-renew).

How the math works

Revenue = price + shipping charged. Fees = listing fee + transaction % × revenue + processing % × revenue + processing fixed. Profit = revenue − fees − item cost − shipping cost. Break-even price is the price where profit is 0.

FAQ

Are these fees right for my country? Defaults are US rates. Payment processing differs by country, so check etsy.com/legal/fees and edit the rates above. Offsite Ads (12–15%) are not included.
