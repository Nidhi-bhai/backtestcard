Sources & method

  • US CPI-U, BLS, via Federal Reserve/BLS public files. US government work, public domain. Annual = mean of 12 monthly values. 2026 = Sep 2026 monthly index.
  • India CPI (2010=100), World Bank WDI FP.CPI.TOTL, originally IMF International Financial Statistics. Licence CC BY 4.0 per World Bank series metadata (License_Type: CC BY-4.0).
  • INR per USD, Federal Reserve H.10, annual mean of monthly averages.

Value in year B = amount × CPI(B)/CPI(A). Average rate = (CPI(B)/CPI(A))^(1/(B−A)) − 1. Numbers are cross-checked against an independent source in tests/hub_check.py.
