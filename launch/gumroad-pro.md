## Description

**See exactly how your portfolio would have survived 2008, 2020 and 2022. Then keep the report.**

BacktestCard is a free, open-source stress tester for Indian investors who measure in dollars. It replays real crashes on your mix of India and US stocks, gold, bonds and cash, with the rupee's effect included.

Pro is for when you want to keep the answer, not just look at it.

**What you get**
- 📄 **A PDF report** of any backtest: your mix, the key results (annual growth, worst fall, worst 12 months, time spent below a previous peak) and every crisis replay, with a share card included. Keep it, print it, or send it to family.
- 📈 **Unlimited CSV uploads** (the free version allows 1). Combine your NIFTY 50 TRI, mutual-fund NAVs and anything else in one test. Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
- 🖼️ **Clean share cards**, without the "Made with BacktestCard" badge.
- 💾 **Saved portfolios.** Name them and reopen them in one click, kept privately in your browser.
- 🔄 **Future Pro features at no extra cost.** SIP/contribution modelling and rolling-period views are next.
