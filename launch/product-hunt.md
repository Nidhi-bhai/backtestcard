**Name:** BacktestCard
**Tagline (60):** Replay real crashes on your India+US portfolio, in USD
**Topics:** Fintech, Investing, Open Source, Personal Finance
**Description (260):** A free, open-source stress tester for Indian investors who think in dollars. Replay 2008, 2020 and 2022 on real public data, with the rupee's effect included, then share a result card or embed it. It runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no tracking. Educational, not advice.

**Maker comment:**
Hi PH! Measured in rupees, India stocks returned 10.8%/yr from 2000 to 2026 (price only). Measured in dollars, it's 7.6%. Most tools can't show you that gap, so I built one that does.
- 7 bundled assets, with data from the Fed, BLS, OECD and World Bank, all fetched from the original publishers.
- Crisis replays with "months to recover".
- Bring your own CSV (NIFTY TRI, a fund NAV), processed locally.
- One-click PNG share card, a share link that holds the full state, and a free embed.
The free tool stays free.
