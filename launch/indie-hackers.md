**Title:** I built a niche backtester for one gap: Indian investors who measure in USD (open source, $0 stack)

**Why this niche:**
- Portfolio Visualizer and testfol.io show demand: hundreds of thousands of visits a month, and people pay $15–55/mo.
- Curvo proved the regional angle by building "the backtester for European index investors."
- Nobody serves India-in-USD.

**What I shipped:**
- A static site on GitHub Pages costing $0/month.
- Share cards and embeds as the growth loop (the "Made with Tally" badge idea).
- An optional one-time Gumroad Pro later.

**The unglamorous part, data licensing:**
- Yahoo, Stooq and NSE are out.
- FRED-hosted copies are out too: their ToS bars caching, even for public-domain series.
- I pull straight from the Fed, BLS, OECD and World Bank instead.
- NIFTY comes only through user upload.

**Numbers so far:** 0 users, $0. I'll post real traffic and conversion numbers every two weeks, good or bad.
