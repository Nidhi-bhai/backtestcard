Measured in rupees, Indian equities returned 10.8% a year from 2000 to 2026 (price index). Measured in US dollars, the same stocks returned 7.6%.

If you're an NRI, you're planning a US education or move, or you simply think in dollars, that gap is real risk most tools never show.

So I built BacktestCard, a free, open-source stress tester that:
• replays 2008, 2013, 2020 and 2022 on your mix of India and US stocks, gold, bonds and cash
• reports results in USD or INR, with currency effects applied consistently
• turns any result into a shareable card or an embed for your blog
• runs entirely in your browser: no sign-up, no data collected

I used only data I'm allowed to redistribute (Federal Reserve, BLS, OECD, World Bank), and the method's limits are written on the page.

It's educational, not investment advice. I'd value feedback from anyone in wealth or fintech.
