How to make a mind map of a book

  1. Write the book's core claim in the centre, in one line.
  2. Use the table of contents to find 3–6 themes. These become branches.
  3. Under each branch, add 2–4 key ideas in your own words. Keep them short, and skip quotes.
  4. Add one "apply" note per branch: what you'll actually do with it.
  5. Review the map a week later and redraw it from memory. That recall is where the learning happens.

FAQ

What is a book mind map?

A one-page diagram with the book's central idea in the middle and its main themes branching out, each holding a few key points in your own words.

How many branches should a book mind map have?

Usually 3–6. More than that tends to mean you're copying the table of contents instead of finding the real structure.

Is mind-mapping better than taking notes?

It forces you to summarise and connect ideas, which helps understanding and recall. Many people use both: the map for structure, the notes for detail.
