Privacy Policy: YT Transcript Copier & Exporter

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This browser extension is made by Nidhi (an independent developer). In short: it collects no personal data, uses no analytics and sells nothing to anyone.

What the extension accesses

  • YouTube pages (youtube.com): when you click copy or export, it reads the caption/transcript data of the video you are viewing, directly from YouTube, inside your browser.
  • Clipboard (write only): to put the transcript on your clipboard when you ask. It never reads your clipboard.
  • Local storage: your settings and, if you buy Pro, your license key and its status are saved locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced to us.

Network requests

  • Requests to youtube.com to fetch the transcript of the current video.
  • Only if you enter a Pro license key: one request to api.gumroad.com to check that the key is valid. This sends the key and the product ID to Gumroad, which processes it under Gumroad's privacy policy.

There are no other servers. The developer does not run a backend and never receives your transcripts, browsing history or viewing activity.

What we do not do

  • No tracking, analytics, ads or cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No collection of personal or sensitive information.

Payments

Pro ($7 one-time) is sold through Gumroad. Gumroad handles your payment and email. The developer receives only the order details Gumroad gives sellers.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored locally.
