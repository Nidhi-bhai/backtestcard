How to copy a YouTube transcript (without timestamps)

Free manual method (desktop browser)

  1. Open the video on youtube.com.
  2. Click …more under the title, scroll down and click Show transcript.
  3. In the transcript panel, open the ⋮ menu and choose Toggle timestamps to hide them.
  4. Click and drag from the first line to the last, then press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac).
  5. Paste into Word or Google Docs with Ctrl+Shift+V (paste as plain text) to avoid messy formatting.

Not every video has a transcript. It only shows if the creator uploaded captions or YouTube auto-generated them.

On iPhone or Android

The YouTube app shows the transcript but doesn't make it easy to select all of it. The simplest route is to open the video in a desktop browser, or use desktop mode in your mobile browser, then follow the steps above.
