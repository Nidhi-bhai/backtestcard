FAQ

How do I make a weekly study schedule?

Decide your fixed study window, split it into focus blocks with short breaks, assign subjects in rotation, and keep one lighter day for review.

How long should a study block be?

25–50 minutes works for most people; use longer blocks for problem-solving subjects if you can stay focused.
