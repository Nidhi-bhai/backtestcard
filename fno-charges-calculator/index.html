What each charge is

  • STT (Securities Transaction Tax): on options it's charged on the sell-side premium, and on futures on the sell-side turnover.
  • Exchange transaction charge: the NSE fee on turnover (premium turnover for options).
  • SEBI turnover fee: ₹10 per crore of turnover.
  • Stamp duty: charged on the buy side only.
  • GST: 18% on brokerage + exchange charge + SEBI fee, not on STT or stamp duty.

Charges look tiny per trade but compound. Hundreds of trades a year at a few hundred rupees each can wipe out a strategy's gross edge, so any honest backtest has to subtract them.

FAQ

How are F&O charges calculated?

Total = brokerage + STT + exchange transaction charge + SEBI fee + stamp duty + 18% GST on (brokerage + exchange + SEBI). Each is a percentage of the relevant turnover, as shown in the calculator.

What is STT on options selling?

STT on options is charged on the premium on the sell side. Rates are revised from time to time, so check the current rate.

Why does my contract note differ slightly?

Brokers round per order, may add IPFT or platform fees, and rates may have changed. Use the editable rates to match your contract note.
