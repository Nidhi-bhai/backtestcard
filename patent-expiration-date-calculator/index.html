US Patent Expiration Date Calculator

Estimate when a US patent stops being enforceable. Enter the dates printed on the patent (or on Google Patents / USPTO Patent Center). This is an estimate for research, not legal advice. Always confirm the status before you rely on it.

Rules the calculator uses

  • Utility patents filed on or after 8 June 1995: 20 years from the earliest US non-provisional filing date (including parent applications it claims priority to), plus any PTA days, cut off by any terminal disclaimer.
  • Utility patents filed before 8 June 1995: the longer of 17 years from grant or 20 years from filing.
  • Design patents: 15 years from grant if filed on or after 13 May 2015, otherwise 14 years from grant. No maintenance fees.
  • Maintenance fees (utility): due 3.5, 7.5 and 11.5 years after grant, with a 6-month grace period. If one isn't paid, the patent lapses early, and many patents lapse this way. Check the fee status in USPTO Patent Center.

How to find expired patents

  1. Search Google Patents for your product category, and filter by priority date older than ~20 years.
  2. Open the result. Google Patents shows an "Expired" or "Lapsed" status label, but treat it as a hint and verify it.
  3. Confirm the maintenance-fee status in USPTO Patent Center.
  4. Check that newer patents don't cover the improvements you'd add. An expired base patent doesn't free up later improvements on it.

FAQ

How long does a US patent last?

Utility patents filed since 8 June 1995 last 20 years from the earliest non-provisional filing date, adjusted by PTA and any terminal disclaimer. Design patents last 15 years from grant (14 if filed before 13 May 2015).

How do I find out if a patent has expired?

Calculate the full term, then check maintenance-fee status in USPTO Patent Center. A missed fee makes a utility patent lapse early.

Can I make a product from an expired patent?

Generally the invention claimed in an expired patent can be used by anyone, but other unexpired patents, trademarks or trade dress may still apply. Get legal advice before you launch.

What is patent term adjustment?

Extra days the USPTO adds to make up for its own delays during examination. The number is printed on the patent's front page.
